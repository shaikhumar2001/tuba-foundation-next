Who We Are

Our Mission & Vision

Grounding personal growth, economic empowerment, and community upliftment in timeless moral principles.

Our Mission

To make modern vocational skills, women empowerment, and value-based education accessible to everyone, so that families can stand on their own feet and together build a self-reliant society grounded in knowledge, dignity, and service.

Modern Skills Women Empowerment Value-Based Education

Our Vision

An educated, skilled, and morally strong community where every individual, whatever their background, has an equal opportunity to learn, grow, and contribute to the well-being of others.

Track 02

Islamic Education & Character Building

Fostering moral grounding, sacred knowledge, and spiritual growth in a supportive and dignified environment.

Madarsa Aayesha Lilbanat 1 Year Comprehensive

1 Year Momina Course

A transformative one-year foundational Islamic scholarship curriculum exclusively for girls and women, cultivating deep spiritual understanding and virtuous character.

  • Quranic Tajweed & Nazra with correct pronunciation
  • Deeniyat, basic Fiqh & essential Islamic duties
  • Sunnah manners, moral ethics & personal character refinement

Community Learning Monthly Curriculum

Monthly Deeni Course

A flexible monthly curriculum created for individuals and families wanting to systematically strengthen their understanding of everyday Islamic faith and practice.

  • Essential beliefs, daily prayers & supplications (Du'as)
  • Virtues of good conduct, family life & community service
